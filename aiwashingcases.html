How to read the evidence: Findings and a guilty plea carry different weight from complaints, settlements, or reporting. A settlement may resolve allegations without admitting liability. “Likely” here means an evidence-backed pattern assessment, conditional where facts remain disputed.

Selection: This is a purposive international sample with substantial U.S. coverage, not a ranking or prevalence estimate. Related aliases are grouped. Delphia and Global Predictions are separate firms with separate orders. Statuses describe the cited dated records, not a certification of every current docket.

Claimed benefits versus documented outcomes. Every case pairs the AI promise with evidence about delivery. Blue identifies the claim; green identifies the outcome and does not imply success. Findings, allegations, and reporting retain their labels.

Numerical evidence: Workado has a claim-versus-test bar chart. The other 99 cases use evidence panels because the cited sources do not supply a matched numerical pair. Missing measurements are never treated as zero. Penalties and funding do not substitute for product output.

Scope: Alongside false capability and earnings claims, the library includes disputed safety claims, corrected benchmarks and misleading AI provenance or affiliation. Genuine AI, disclosed human assistance and legitimate use of open-source components are not themselves evidence of deception. Read each assessment and response.
